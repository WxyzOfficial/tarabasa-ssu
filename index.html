Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to answer a form?
No. You don't register or sign in. Just open the form link and answer. Your session is managed anonymously in the background.
Can I submit more than once?
That depends on how the DSWD administrator set up the form. Some forms allow multiple submissions; others don't. If you're blocked, contact the form owner.
Who can see my answers?
Only the administrator who created the form can view your submission. Other respondents can never see your answers. Access is enforced by Firestore security rules, not just client-side code.
What if the form is closed or expired?
You'll see a clear message letting you know the form is no longer accepting responses. The administrator may be able to re-open it for you.
Can I edit my answers after submitting?
By default, no. If the form is configured to allow edits, you'll see an option after submitting. Otherwise, contact the administrator to submit a corrected response.
I lost the form link. What do I do?
Ask the DSWD administrator who shared the form with you to resend the link. If you have the form ID, you can also paste it into the "Find a Form by ID" box above.
Is my data protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012?
Yes. In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines, personal information is handled responsibly and protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore with access controls to help protect your information. Only authorized DSWD administrators may access submissions for their forms, and only the form owner may modify or delete a form. Personal information is collected and processed only for legitimate purposes and in accordance with applicable data privacy principles.
