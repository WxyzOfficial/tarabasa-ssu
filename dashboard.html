How It Works

Four simple steps from building a form to analyzing responses.

1

Build Your Form

Add questions, set options, and customize the design in the admin dashboard.

2

Publish & Share

Click publish, copy the unique link, and share it with respondents via email or social media.

3

Collect Responses

Respondents submit answers without needing an account — no signup required.

4

Manage in Spreadsheet

Open the built-in spreadsheet to search, filter, sort, and export responses.

For Administrators

  • Manage unlimited forms from one dashboard
  • Duplicate, edit, and reorganize forms instantly
  • Open or close forms without deleting them
